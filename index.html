How to use Speed Lab

  1. Pick a car from the garage. Each color has its own steady speed — except one, which speeds up as it goes.
  2. Drag the checkered flag (or tap the ruler) to choose how far the car must travel.
  3. Press Start. The car drives and the stopwatch times it. When it reaches the flag, the point (time, distance) appears on the graph in that car's color.
  4. Run the same car at several distances to draw its line. Then compare cars.

Your mission: Which car is accelerating? Constant-speed cars make straight lines. The accelerating car makes a curve. 🧐
